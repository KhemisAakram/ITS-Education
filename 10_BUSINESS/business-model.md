# Business Model

## Initial revenue streams
- Weekly courses
- Workshops
- Camps
- Intensive programs

## Future revenue streams
- Electronics kits
- Robotics kits
- Experiment kits
- Books and project guides
- Events and competitions
- Engineering and prototyping services
- Robotics and automation projects
- PCB and IoT development

## Pilot principle
The first pilot should validate the education model and the economics simultaneously.

## Customer groups
Primary: parents seeking practical technology education for children and teenagers.

Future: schools, educational organizations, advanced students, and engineering/technology customers.

## Development model
**One laboratory → repeatable course → additional classes → additional laboratory → products/partnerships → broader technology ecosystem**

## Financial rule
Do not assume profitability from tuition alone. Measure actual teacher time, equipment consumption, consumables, operating overhead, enrollment, attendance, cancellations, and cash timing.
