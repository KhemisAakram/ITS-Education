# Institute Vision

## Vision
Create a children's technology and engineering institute where students develop the ability to understand, build, program, design, and improve real technological systems.

## Long-term development
1. Local education center
2. Technology institute
3. Regional institute
4. Educational product company
5. Technology & engineering ecosystem

## What we are building
A long-term development pathway for young technology creators. Students progressively learn to observe, experiment, build, program, measure, debug, understand, design, and create.

## Desired student journey
**Curiosity → Discovery → Making → Programming → Electronics → Robotics → Engineering → Design → Product Development → Innovation**

## Success
Students should increasingly be able to explain systems, build circuits, write programs, measure, diagnose problems, modify systems, design new systems, work independently, document work, present projects, and build complete products.

## Educational objective
Move students from "I know how to use technology" toward **"I understand technology and I can create it."**
