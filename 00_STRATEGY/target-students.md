# Target Students

The institute is designed primarily for ages 6–17. Age ranges are guidelines; placement should consider experience, knowledge, practical ability, maturity, and learning speed.

## Explorer — 6–8
Objective: **"I can make something happen."**
Focus: simple electricity, circuits, LEDs, buttons, motors, simple sensors, visual programming, mechanisms.
Projects: LED flashlight, traffic light, electronic dice, alarm, push-button game, mini fan, light sensor, simple robot.

## Maker — 8–11
Objective: **"I understand how the things I build work."**
Focus: voltage, current, resistance, LEDs, switches, potentiometers, transistors, buzzers, sensors, motors, breadboards, multimeters, Scratch, Arduino.
Projects: automatic night light, temperature monitor, electronic safe, obstacle detector, mini robot, reaction-time game, smart plant system.

## Junior Engineer — 11–14
Objective: **"I can design a system."**
Focus: Ohm's law, transistors, MOSFETs, PWM, ADC, sensors, motor control, power supplies, communication protocols, C/C++, Python, Arduino, ESP32, robotics, CAD, IoT.
Projects: robotic arm, autonomous vehicle, IoT device, smart home, wireless sensor, electronic instrument, automated machine.

## Young Engineer — 14–17
Objective: **"I can design and build a real product."**
Focus: embedded systems, PCB design, ESP32/STM32, electronics design, control systems, robotics, Python, C/C++, CAD, communication, sensors, motors, power electronics, debugging, product development.
Projects: custom PCB, robot, CNC system, IoT product, automated machine, autonomous robot, measurement device, wireless charging system.

## Progression
Advance through demonstrated competence in knowledge, practical skills, problem solving, safety, project completion, debugging, communication, and independence.
