# Teacher Guide — Level 1

## Purpose
Create a consistent teaching system for practical, project-driven learning.

## Teacher roles
- Instructor
- Facilitator
- Debugging coach
- Safety supervisor
- Mentor

## Teacher behavior
Prefer questions over immediately supplying solutions.

Useful prompts:
- What do you observe?
- What should happen?
- What is different?
- What is the simplest thing we can test?
- What changed after your test?
- What is your hypothesis?

## Debugging model
**Observe → Describe → Hypothesize → Test → Identify → Correct → Test again**

## Standard 90-minute session
1. Concept — 10 min
2. Demonstration — 15 min
3. Guided experiment — 20 min
4. Project — 35 min
5. Presentation/debugging — 10 min

## Before class
Prepare workstations, components, code/materials, safety equipment, project examples, and known failure points.

## Classroom routine
Arrival → safety reminder → concept → demonstration → experiment → project → testing/debugging → explanation → cleanup.

## Independence
Progress students from guided work toward modifying instructions, solving defined problems, designing solutions, and defining their own projects.

## Differentiation
- Support: additional guidance and checkpoints
- Core: standard project
- Extension: redesign, optimization, or additional function

## Teamwork
Suggested rotating roles:
- Builder
- Programmer
- Tester
- Documenter

## Safety culture
Safety is taught as an engineering skill. Stop unsafe behavior, explain why, and restart safely.

## Assessment
Use evidence of what students can build, explain, test, debug, and improve.

## Completion question
**What can the student now build, explain, test, debug, and improve?**
