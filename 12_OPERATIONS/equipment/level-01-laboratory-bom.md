# Level 1 Laboratory BOM Framework

## Pilot target
- 10–12 students
- 5–6 workstations
- 2 students/workstation
- shared specialist equipment

## Planned quantities
- 6 workstations
- 12 breadboards
- 12 Arduino-compatible boards
- 6 multimeters
- 12–18 DC motors
- 12–18 servos
- 6 robot chassis
- 6–12 motor drivers
- 20 light sensors
- 10–12 temperature sensors
- 6–10 distance sensors
- 100+ LEDs
- 100+ resistors
- 30–50 buttons
- 20–30 potentiometers
- 15–20 buzzers
- 15–20 USB cables
- 6–12 power solutions
- 1 shared oscilloscope
- 2 soldering stations

## Additional categories
Storage, safety equipment, mechanical materials, tools, consumables, component kits, and inventory identification.

## Purchasing principles
Prioritize teaching-critical items, track alternatives and suppliers, and keep approximately 10–20% spare stock for critical components where appropriate.

## Inventory
Track item ID, category, quantity, minimum stock, reorder point, safety stock, consumption, damage/loss, and replacement cost.

## Important status
Exact part numbers, suppliers, and prices are not frozen. These must be researched before purchasing.
