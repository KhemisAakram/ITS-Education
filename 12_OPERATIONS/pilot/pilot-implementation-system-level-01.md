# Level 1 Pilot Implementation System

## Pilot model
- 10–12 students
- 5–6 workstations
- 1 primary teacher
- Optional assistant
- 24 sessions × 90 minutes
- 36 classroom hours
- 12 weeks × 2 sessions/week

## Workstation teams
Two students per workstation, with rotating roles:
- Builder
- Programmer
- Tester
- Documenter

## Operating system
Standardize preparation, arrival, safety, teaching, equipment checkout, project work, cleanup, attendance, and documentation.

## Equipment and damage
Track equipment checkout and damaged components. Record the cause where known and update replacement/spare assumptions.

## Parent communication
Maintain appropriate communication about attendance, progress, projects, and course milestones.

## Student portfolio
Collect evidence progressively rather than only at the end.

## Pilot issue log
Track educational, operational, equipment, staffing, parent, student, and financial issues.

## Pilot metrics
- Educational progress
- Project completion
- Attendance
- Student independence
- Parent feedback
- Teacher workload
- Equipment consumption
- Damage/loss
- Financial performance

## Review points
Mid-pilot review at Session 12 and final review after Session 24.

## Principle
The first class is both a class and a test of the institute's operating system.
