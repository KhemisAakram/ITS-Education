# Assessment System — Level 1

## Philosophy
Assessment measures demonstrated competence and growth rather than attendance or copying.

## Competence levels
1. **Guided** — completes with significant assistance
2. **Developing** — completes with some assistance
3. **Capable** — completes and explains independently
4. **Independent Maker** — designs, debugs, and improves with limited assistance

## Domains
1. Electronics
2. Sensors
3. Programming
4. Actuators
5. Robotics
6. Engineering
7. Debugging
8. Communication

## Evidence
Assess through projects, demonstrations, explanations, notebooks, tests, debugging, and final work.

## Standard project assessment
Evaluate:
- Understanding
- Build quality and function
- Safe working
- Testing
- Debugging
- Improvement
- Communication
- Independence

## Independence tracking
Record progression using:
- G — Guided
- P — Progressing
- I — Independent

## Final assessment
Includes:
- practical demonstration
- final engineering project
- student explanation
- debugging evidence
- reflection

## Completion
A student completes Level 1 when the required competencies are demonstrated through sufficient evidence, not merely because all sessions were attended.
